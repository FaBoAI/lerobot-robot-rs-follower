# v0.0.15

- Increased post-contact gripper pressure scale from `2.5` to `3.0`.
- Increased the effective post-contact position-error safety cap from `0.10 rad` to `0.12 rad`.
- Free-closing speed, normal motor gains, contact detection, and opening behavior are unchanged.
- This is a 20% increase in the post-contact hold limit compared with v0.0.14.
